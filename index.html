Privacy Policy

App Name: Tic Tac Toe Online

Developer: ARGHYA BOSE

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Tic Tac Toe Online app handles user information.

2. Information Collection and Use

This app does not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information such as name, email address, phone number, location, or contacts.

For online multiplayer gameplay, the app may temporarily process non-personal game data such as: player moves, game state, and room identifiers. This data is used only to enable gameplay and is not linked to any individual.

3. Data Storage

  • No personal data is stored permanently.
  • Game-related data exists only during active sessions and is removed afterward.

4. Third-Party Services

The app may use services (such as Firebase) for real-time multiplayer support. These services may collect minimal technical data required for operation, governed by their own privacy policies.

5. Internet Permission

The app requires internet to enable remote multiplayer gameplay.

6. Children’s Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Data Security

All data transmitted during gameplay is encrypted in transit for security.
